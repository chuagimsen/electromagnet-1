How to use this simulator 1. Click Close switch S to start the animation. 2. Watch the circuit and read the explanation on the right as each stage is shown. 3. You can use Previous Stage and Next Stage to move through the stages manually, or click Auto Animate to let the circuit run automatically.

1

When the switch is closed, the L shaped bar touches the bottom metal contacts and electric current flows through the closed circuit. P and Q light up.

2

The fixed iron bar becomes an electromagnet/ gets magnetised.

3

The electromagnet attracts the iron bar and the L shaped bar moved vertically up to B. Electric current stops flowing through the outer loop.

4

At B, the right side of the iron bar touches the two right metal contacts. The electromagnet demagnetised and electric current only flows through Q in a closed circuit. Q lights up and not P.

5

The fixed iron bar drops back to A and touches the bottom metal contacts again. The cycle repeats and the iron bar moved up and down.

Use Previous Stage and Next Stage for manual control, or press Auto Animate to run the stages automatically. While auto animation is running, press Pause Auto to stop at the current stage and Resume Auto to continue.
