1

When the switch is closed, the iron bar at A touches the bottom metal contacts and electric current flows through the closed circuit. P and Q light up.

2

The fixed iron cylinder becomes an electromagnet/ gets magnetised.

3

The electromagnet attracts the iron bar and the iron bar moves vertically up to B. Electric current stops flowing through the outer loop.

4

At B, the right side of the iron bar touches the two right metal contacts. The electromagnet demagnetises and electric current only flows through Q in a closed circuit. Q lights up and not P.

5

The iron bar drops back to A and touches the bottom metal contacts again. The cycle repeats and the iron bar moves up and down.

When P and Q are both lit in series, they are shown at the same brightness. When Q is lit on its own, its brighter flashing rays make the change visible.
